# POETH v0.1.0

**POE Trade Helper (POETH)** is a free helper for Path of Exile that sits on top of the game. Hover an item, press
`Ctrl+D` and see a fair price. Trading, currency exchange, wealth tracking and a leveling guide live in one app.
It works with both the English and the Russian game client. This is the first release for PoE 1; PoE 2 gets the
campaign tracker and item prices.

## Highlights

- **Item price with `Ctrl+D`** right at the cursor, plus a hint on what to do with the item: sell it to a vendor,
  save it up to sell in bulk, or list it for ~X. Copy the price note with one button.
- **Rare items are searched precisely:** on the same base, with the valuable mods already checked. “Market price”
  shows the cheapest similar listings, without bait.
- **Currency is priced by the exchange rate at Faustus** (hourly GGG data), not by stale trade-site listings.
- **In-game trade window** (`Ctrl+Shift+T`) with favorite folders and Better Trading import, and **live search**
  with alerts over the game.
- **Trades:** incoming and outgoing trade cards, stash cell highlight, PoE Hub blacklist. **Chat commands on
  hotkeys:** `F5` — hideout.
- **Exchange:** what is worth buying and selling at Faustus, at realistic buy and sell prices with the gold fee.
  Read the “Market Ratio” table from the screen with `Alt+D` (beta).
- **Wealth:** what your stash is worth and what you gained; **map tracker** — time, deaths and income per hour.
- **Regex strings** for your stash and vendors: map mods, item properties, your own library on hotkeys.
- **PoE 1 leveling guide** over the game (`Ctrl+Shift+G`): the step moves on by itself when you enter a new zone.
- **English and Russian client:** item and currency names follow the game's language, explanations follow the
  interface language. A simple mode for newcomers and an expert mode.

## How to install

1. Download `POETH-Setup-0.1.0.exe` below and run it. POETH installs into your user folder, no administrator
   rights needed.
2. Windows shows “Windows protected your PC” — click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. That is how Windows
   greets programs without a paid code-signing certificate.
3. The first-run wizard helps you pick the language, the game and the trade site. Set the game to
   **Windowed Fullscreen** or **Windowed**.

No install: `POETH-Portable-0.1.0.exe` runs right away; download new versions yourself.

Something not working — see the [FAQ](https://github.com/MAYZOSUPER/POETH/blob/main/docs/FAQ.en.md).

## Known limitations

- **League 3.30 (starts November 13).** POETH switches to the new league by itself. It picks up new item mods
  from the trade site within a day. Full 3.30 data arrives as an update without reinstalling once the new league
  data is out: judging by past leagues, within the first 1–3 days. Until then some new items may be “not
  recognized”. When the “Data updated” toast appears, restart POETH. “Check for updates” must be on in Settings
  (it is on by default).
- **Reading the “Market Ratio” table from the screen is in beta.** Recognition can be wrong, especially at a
  different resolution or Windows scaling. Doubtful cells are flagged as “cells to check” and stay out of the math
  until you confirm them. Compare the numbers with the game.
- **PoE 2:** only the campaign tracker and item prices for now. The trade window, live search, trades, regex,
  Exchange, Wealth and the leveling guide are PoE 1 only.
- **Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only.** POETH windows are not visible while the game is in exclusive fullscreen.
- **The portable version** does not update itself and does not show Windows notifications; alerts over the game
  work as usual.
